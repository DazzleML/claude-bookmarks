## High-Value Verification (~5 minutes)

### HV.1 — Claude's own reference has no anchor glyph

- [ ] **Steps:** ask Claude to point you to an earlier message with its `bookmark` tool, for its own reference.
- [ ] **Expected:** the link in its reply is drawn without `⚓`; a click jumps there with the words highlighted, as before.

### HV.2 — A bookmark for you keeps the glyph

- [ ] **Steps:** ask Claude to bookmark a message for you.
- [ ] **Expected:** the link carries `⚓`, and the bookmark appears in your list (`Ctrl+] b`).

### HV.3 — Claim

- [ ] **Steps:** ask Claude to claim one of its references for you; open `Ctrl+] b`.
- [ ] **Expected:** it is now in your list, the same link still jumps to the same place, and it is gone from Claude's list.

### HV.3b — Claim from the list

- [ ] **Steps:** `Ctrl+] b`, `l` to Claude's group, pick one with its number (or `j`/`k`), press `c`.
- [ ] **Expected:** a toast says it is yours now; the band's hint lists `c claim`; it shows in your group. On one of yours, `c` says it is already on your list.

### HV.4 — Older bookmarks unchanged

- [ ] **Steps:** open a conversation with bookmarks from before 0.4.20.
- [ ] **Expected:** their links still carry `⚓`.
