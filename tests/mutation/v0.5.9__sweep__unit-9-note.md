## Triage

- **M2, killable.** The tie-break (earliest `createdAt` among equal owner rank and tier) had no fixture: two records of one owner and tier on one message cannot coexist (a relabel finds the first), so the tie needs two *open* owners (`agent:`/`person:`). Added to the primary-record test.
- **M8, killable.** The parser's "a key has no whitespace" rule had no fixture; `two words: not a key` added.
- **M10, killable.** The flow list's empty-item filter had no fixture; `[a, , b]` added.

No equivalents; `equivalents.md` unchanged.

## Caveats

A kill proves the suite noticed *this* change, not that it tests the right thing. **A clean sweep is evidence about the mutations tried, not proof of suite completeness.** In particular no mutant touches Obsidian's own reading of the YAML the writer emits, or its stale-buffer behaviour when the plugin rewrites a note it has open: those are the checklist's (`tests/checklists/v0.5.9__Feature__the-markdown-as-an-obsidian-note.md`), in a person's hands. The red-green audit of the seven new tests (7 anchors / 0 guards, `scratchpad/redgreen/audit9.py`) is the separate check that those tests are attached to the change.
