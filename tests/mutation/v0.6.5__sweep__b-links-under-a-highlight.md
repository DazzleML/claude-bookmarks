## Caveats

A kill proves the suite noticed *this* change, not that it tests the right thing, and **a clean sweep is evidence about the mutations tried, not proof of suite completeness.** Whether a Box's background paints behind a Markdown child in a real terminal is not a thing any of these tests can see; it is the A6 checklist's live look.
