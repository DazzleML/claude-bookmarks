## Triage

- **M4, killable.** The title is the person's words and round-trips verbatim; the fixture had none to lose.
- **M8, killable.** `exportMarkdown` skips empty strings so a `why: ''` never becomes a `why:` line; nothing exercised an empty value.
- **M10, M11, killable.** `headerFields` and `conversationTitle` had no kit test at all (the red-green audit's R2 found the same gap); the kit now seeds a cwd with a trailing slash and two prompts.

## Caveats

A kill proves the suite noticed *this* change, not that it tests the right thing. **A clean sweep is evidence about the mutations tried, not proof of suite completeness.** The red-green audit of the unit's own tests (4 anchors / 2 guards, `scratchpad/redgreen/audit6.py`) is the separate check that those tests are attached to the change.
