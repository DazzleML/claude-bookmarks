## Triage

- **M3, killable.** The fixture had one unnumbered record, so the `createdAt` tie-break among unnumbered records never decided anything; a second, older one was added and the order asserted.
- **M12, equivalent.** `rebuildIndex` skips `index.json` by name before parsing; without the skip, `parseRegister` refuses it anyway (no `sessionId`, no `perma` array), so no entry is made and the files written are identical. The skip stays as a statement of intent and saves one parse. Recorded in `equivalents.md` under the handler's hash.

## Caveats

A kill proves the suite noticed *this* change, not that it tests the right thing. **A clean sweep is evidence about the mutations tried, not proof of suite completeness.** Nothing here exercises Obsidian's rendering of INDEX.md or its relative links; that is a line in unit 12's checklist. The red-green audit of the six new tests (6 anchors / 0 guards, `scratchpad/redgreen/audit10.py`) is the separate check that those tests are attached to the change.
