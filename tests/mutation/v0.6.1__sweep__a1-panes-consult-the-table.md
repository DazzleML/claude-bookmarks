## Caveats

A kill proves the suite noticed *this* change, not that it tests the right thing, and a clean sweep is evidence about the mutations tried, not proof of suite completeness. The views (`prompts-pane.tsx`, `bookmarks-pane.tsx`, `band.tsx`) were not mutation targets: their change is a routing that keeps the drawing byte-identical, pinned by the kit's goldens and the three `pane-keys` fences; the red-green audit of the same day recorded those three as guards by design.
