## Anomalies

M7's first anchor matched two lines (the refusal and the catch block's log both begin "README.md not written"); the harness refused to apply it rather than guess, and the mutant ran in round 2 with the distinguishing words included. A harness that applied an ambiguous anchor to the first match would have produced a verdict about the wrong line.

## Caveats

A kill proves the suite noticed *this* change, not that it tests the right thing, and **a clean sweep is evidence about the mutations tried, not proof of suite completeness.** The byte-identity test holds the README's text to what it was on 2026-10-21; a deliberate wording change later means regenerating the fixture, on purpose.
