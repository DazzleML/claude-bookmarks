## Caveats

A kill proves the suite noticed *this* change, not that it tests the right thing. A clean second round is evidence about the mutations tried, not proof of suite completeness. In particular the thing this unit is for, the terminal cutting a row at the pane's edge, is invisible to the kit (it renders a tree, not columns): that is a live check on A6's checklist, not anything these mutants reach.
